# Weapons

What each weapon is like to fight with. Every swing's timing, damage and reach is on its entry.

## sword

The quick, all-round weapon every settler starts with, and the root of the Swordsmanship tree: a fast combo of wide swings that ends in a heavy third blow. Its tree can widen every swing to hit a crowd ([[skills/cleave]]) and turn the last hit into a spin that hits everyone around you ([[skills/whirlwind]]).

## club

A heavy combo of two swings, slower to start and to recover than the sword's. It is the root of the Restoration tree, carried alongside [[skills/heal|Healing Circle]] and the [[movement/block]].

## staff

A single quick jab: the staff is the root of the Pyromancy tree, and what you swing while you wait to call down a [[abilities/meteor]].

## bow

Shoots arrows that fly fast and straight, so you can hit what you can't reach. It is the root of the Archery tree, which quickens the draw ([[skills/rapidFire]]) and sends arrows through more than one foe ([[skills/piercing]]).
