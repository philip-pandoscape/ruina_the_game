# House Blueprints

Each house you can build, in words. Its stages, cost and tax are on its entry.

## cottage

A small timber-and-plaster house for one settler. Buy the blueprint at the Land Registry, claim a plot at [[places/hearthside]] with it, and raise it stage by stage by hauling stone blocks, lumber bundles and iron fittings to the site. Finished, it gives you a bed where labor comes back faster, a storage chest, and a home to wake in.
