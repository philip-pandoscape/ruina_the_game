# People

Who each of the island's people is. What they sell, make and ask of you is on their entry.

## hollis

Reeve Hollis, the village elder, keeps Lanternwick by the well in its square. He welcomes every newcomer off the morning boat and starts them on the island's work: a patch of earth, a scarecrow, and seeds to put in it. Most of the first quests are his.

## mara

Mara Thistle keeps the grocer's stall in Lanternwick: seeds and saplings to sow, and coin for your crops, herbs and animal produce. She brews [[items/tonic.labor|labor tonics]] from herbs, too.

## tamsin

Tamsin Hale, the packwright, works the [[workbenches/lanternwickBench]] in the square. Talk to her to pack your produce into trade packs. Her rule of trade: a pack sold where it was made is worth nothing.

## bertram

Bertram Cogg, the wainwright, builds carts in his workshop: a farm cart for the patient, a roadster for the bold, a hauler for the ambitious. Bring him timber, iron and coin and he makes you the deed.

## sela

Sela Brightmane keeps the stables. She sells the whistles that call a [[mounts/donkey]] or a [[mounts/horse]], and the young animals you raise in a plot: chicks, lambs and calves.

## ilsa

Captain Ilsa Marrow, the shipwright, works the shed by the docks. She builds rowboats and sloops to order, and sells the design for the [[ships/cog]], a ship too big to build in a shed: it goes up on the slipway.

## quill

Registrar Quill keeps the Land Registry, where land is granted by blueprint and kept by paying tax. He sells scarecrows and house blueprints, and takes the tax on your house.

## ysolde

Ysolde Windward, the wingwright, teaches the way into the air: jump, jump again, then once more to open your wings. She sells the swifter of the island's gliders.

## dunmore

Dunmore Vell keeps the trading post at [[places/westwatch]], on the western headland. He buys the packs the east of the island makes; the more he buys of a kind, the less he pays for the next, until time restores his appetite. His board has contracts too.

## pell

Factor Pell runs the [[places/gullrock]] exchange on its islet off the coast, a long row from anywhere. That is why he pays in Charter Seals as well as silver.

## hob

Hob Ashgrove, the woodcutter, lives at his camp in [[places/thornwood]]. Chop the deadfall round his camp for lumber and bundle it at his [[workbenches/sawhorse]]; he sells lumber himself, and buys it, and pays well for a thunderstruck log.

## brenna

Brenna Stoneheart, the quarrymaster, keeps the stores at [[places/quarry]]: stone and iron ore, raw or ready, and the [[workbenches/stonecutter]] where they become blocks and crates of fittings.
