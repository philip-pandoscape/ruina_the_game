# Regions

Each region of the world, in words. Its numbers, places and people are on its entry.

## emberholm

The starter isle, a green island at the foot of the world tree, built so that every way of travelling has somewhere to go. The village sits on its harbour; farmland lies east of it and house plots on a rise to the west; the Windspire crag stands to the north with a ledge path to its top; forest and a quarry hold its timber and stone; and two trading posts can only be reached the long way round, one by road and cliff, one by sea.
