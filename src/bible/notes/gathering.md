# Gathering

What each kind of gathering spot is. Its yield, labor and how soon it comes back are on its entry.

## timberNode

Deadfall timber lying in the forest round Hob's camp in [[places/thornwood]]. Chop it for [[items/lumber]]; now and then a log turns out to have been split by lightning long ago, a [[items/thunderLog]] worth a small fortune.

## stoneNode

Pale rock outcrops at [[places/quarry]]. Quarry them for [[items/stone]], to be cut into blocks at the [[workbenches/stonecutter]].

## oreNode

Rust-streaked outcrops at [[places/quarry]], mined for [[items/ironOre]]: the nails, fittings and axles of everything you build.

## herbNode

Patches of wild herbs. Pick them for [[items/herbs]], which [[npcs/mara]] brews into labor tonics.
