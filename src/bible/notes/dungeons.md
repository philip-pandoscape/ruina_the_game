# Dungeons

Each dungeon, in words. How copies are kept and parties formed is on [[systems/parties]].

## goblinsDread

The lair of the Split-Eye goblins, inside the Windspire. Its way in is a portal on a ledge behind the waterfall on the crag's south-west face, under a hood of rock, beside the goblins' landing stage, nets and totem: only a glider reaches it. Each party that goes in plays its own copy of the inner grotto.
