# Creatures

Each creature, in words. Its numbers are on its entry, and how it thinks is on [[systems/creatures]].

## dummy

A training dummy on a post: it stands still, takes your hits, wobbles and shows the damage, and is back up a moment after it falls. It never hits back.

## slime

The meadow's slimes leave you alone until you hit one. Then the pack fights as one: the slime you hit calls the others in while it backs away from you, and once enough are close it gathers them up and grows a size. Anything angry and close winds up and slams the ground where you stood. Kill one and it bursts into slimelets.

## slimelet

What a slime bursts into when it dies: small, quick and fragile, running from you while a fuse burns. Kill them in time; any left alive when the fuse runs out come back together as a slime one size bigger than the one you killed.

## great-slime

A slime grown from gathering others: bigger, tougher, with a wider slam, and it can gather again. It bursts into more slimelets than a slime does.

## slime-monarch

The biggest a slime grows: slow, enormous, with a slam that covers a wide ring of ground. It no longer gathers, and its burst is the largest of all.

## goblin

The basic fighter of the Split-Eye war-band. Goblins fight as a band, not one by one: they take turns to come at you, circle you, look for an opening, stab when they are close and throw stones when they are not, and they lose their nerve when the band is beaten.
