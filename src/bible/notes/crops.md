# Crops

What each crop is for. Its numbers are on its entry, drawn from the content.

## sunroot

The quickest thing you can grow: golden tubers that ripen while you walk to the workbench and back. Sunroot fills most of a [[packs/harvestCrate]] and a [[packs/hamper]], and sells to [[npcs/mara]] when you need coin fast.

## barley

Bearded grain, the island's staple. It goes into the [[packs/harvestCrate]] beside sunroot, and it is what every farm animal eats: [[animals/chicken|chickens]], [[animals/sheep|sheep]] and [[animals/cow|cows]] all want a feed of barley.

## flax

Slender stems topped with pale-blue flowers, grown for their fibre. Flax is baled into the [[packs/flaxBale]] to sell, and woven into [[packs/sailcloth]] for the rigging of a ship built at the slipway.

## emberberry

A low bush of glowing orange-red berries from warmer lands. It grows slowly on Emberholm, out of its climate, but its berries make [[packs/emberPreserves]], the dearest pack the island makes.

## apple

A tree, not a crop: it takes its time to grow, then fruits again and again for as long as you leave it standing. Apples make the [[packs/ciderCask]] and go into [[packs/emberPreserves]]; when you are done with the tree, fell it for [[items/lumber]].

## pine

A timber tree from frost country. It bears nothing to pick: grow it, then fell it for [[items/lumber]], with the same rare chance of a [[items/thunderLog]] as any tree you fell.
