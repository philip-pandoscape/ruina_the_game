# Places

What is at each place on the island. Who works there, and the routes that run there, are on its entry.

## lanternwick

The harbour village where you arrive: a cobbled square with a well, market stalls and the specialty workbench, ringed by red-tiled houses, the inn, the wainwright's workshop, the stables and the Land Registry. Most of the island's people and most of its first quests are here.

## docks

Two piers and the shipwright's shed on Lanternwick's harbour, with the slipway where ships are built. Summon boats here; the bay opens south to the sea.

## barleybrook

Open, level farmland beside the villagers' own fields: where most settlers set their first scarecrow.

## hearthside

House plots on a rise above the village, waiting for a blueprint and the materials to raise a house.

## windspire

A tall crag with a ledge path spiralling up its face and the glider launch on top; rising air at its foot carries a glider back up. A waterfall spills down its south-west face, and behind the falling water, on a ledge only a glider reaches, is the way into [[dungeons/goblinsDread]].

## westwatch

The trading post on the western headland, reached by the west road and up the cliffs. It buys Lanternwick's trade packs at the plain route price.

## gullrock

An exchange on an islet off the south-east coast, reachable only by boat or a long glide. The far route pays more, and in more Charter Seals.

## thornwood

Dense forest where deadfall timber lies for the taking, round Hob's camp and his sawhorse.

## quarry

A stone pit with pale stone and rust-streaked iron outcrops, Brenna's stores and the stonecutter's table.

## meadow

Open grass where a slime pack lives, beside a training dummy to practise on. The slimes leave you alone until you hit one.

## strand

A sandy beach on a sheltered bay: a good place to learn to swim and to row.
