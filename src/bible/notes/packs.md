# Packs

What each pack is for. Its recipe, price and routes are on its entry, drawn from the content.

## harvestCrate

The first pack most settlers make: a slatted crate of sunroot and barley, packed at the [[workbenches/lanternwickBench]]. Cheap to fill from your own plot, and the pack the questline teaches trade with.

## flaxBale

Pale flax fibre wrapped in sacking and roped tight. A steady seller made from a single crop, so a plot of [[crops/flax]] alone keeps you in bales.

## emberPreserves

Jars of glowing emberberry jam packed in straw. Emberberries grow slowly on Emberholm, which is why these fetch the most of any pack Lanternwick makes.

## ciderCask

A banded oak cask of Redcap cider, pressed from the apples of trees you have grown. It takes patience: the trees must grow before they fruit.

## fleeceBale

Cream fleece from your sheep, bound in sacking. The pack that pays you back for raising [[animals/sheep]].

## hamper

A wicker hamper of eggs in straw, a jug of milk and a few tubers: a little of everything a farm with animals makes.

## lumberBundle

Planks stacked and lashed at [[workbenches/sawhorse]] in Thornwood. The material every house and ship needs most of; it sells nowhere, it builds.

## stoneBlock

A squared building stone cut at the [[workbenches/stonecutter]]. Houses stand on it.

## ironCrate

A heavy crate of nails, hinges and brackets made from iron ore at the [[workbenches/stonecutter]]. Roofs and hulls need fittings.

## sailcloth

Flax woven into a thick roll of sailcloth at the [[workbenches/lanternwickBench]], for rigging a ship at the slipway.
