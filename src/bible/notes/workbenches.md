# Workbenches

What each workbench is for. What it makes, from what, is on its entry.

## lanternwickBench

The specialty workbench in Lanternwick's square, kept by [[npcs/tamsin]]. Every trade pack the island makes is packed here from local produce, and so is sailcloth. A pack made here sells for nothing here: it has to travel.

## sawhorse

Hob's sawhorse at his camp in [[places/thornwood]], where [[npcs/hob]] works. Bring lumber and bundle it into [[packs/lumberBundle|lumber bundles]] to haul to a building site or the slipway.

## stonecutter

The stonecutter's table at [[places/quarry]], kept by [[npcs/brenna]]. Stone becomes [[packs/stoneBlock|stone blocks]] and iron ore becomes [[packs/ironCrate|crates of fittings]] here.
