# Animals

What each farm animal is for. Its numbers are on its entry, drawn from the content.

## chicken

Bought as a chick from [[npcs/sela]] and raised in a plot cell like a crop. Fed on [[items/barley]], it grows into a hen that lays [[items/egg|eggs]] for as long as it is fed; eggs go into the [[packs/hamper]].

## sheep

Raised from a lamb on [[items/barley]]. A grown sheep is shorn for [[items/wool]] again and again while it is fed, and wool is baled into the [[packs/fleeceBale]].

## cow

Raised from a calf on [[items/barley]]. A grown cow gives [[items/milk]] while it is fed; milk travels best in a [[packs/hamper]], sold far from home.
