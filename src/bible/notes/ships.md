# Ship Designs

Each ship built at a slipway, in words. Its stages and what they take are on its entry.

## cog

The Merchant Cog: a broad-bellied trading ship with a square sail that loves a following wind, and room in her hold for more packs than anything else that floats. She is too big to buy: buy her design from [[npcs/ilsa]], lay her keel on the Lanternwick slipway, and build her up stage by stage from hauled lumber, iron and sailcloth until she launches and her deed is yours.
