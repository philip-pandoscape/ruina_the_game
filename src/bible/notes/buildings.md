# Buildings

What each building and piece of street furniture is. Its size, what blocks you and where it stands are on its entry.

## cottage

The house you build yourself on a plot at [[places/hearthside]], from the [[blueprints/cottage]]: it rises a stage at a time, from its foundation to its roof, as you haul the materials to the site. Finished, it is your home: a bed to rest in and a chest to keep things in.

## townhouse

A two-storey village house: rough stone below, timber-framed plaster above, under red tiles. Its door is open and its rooms are furnished, so you can walk in and up the stairs.

## rowhouse

A narrow house gabled to the street, two storeys of timber and plaster, with a shop below and rooms above.

## longhouse

One long stone room under one roof, with a hearth at one end and beds at the other.

## inn

The Lantern & Gull, Lanternwick's inn: a stone common room with a hearth, a bar and its kegs, tables under chandeliers, and rooms upstairs.

## stall

An open market stall in the square: a counter across the front, posts and a striped awning, crates of produce on the counter. It blocks you only behind its counter.

## workbench

The heavy oak workbench where trade packs are made, with a vice, tools on a rail and a half-packed crate. Each of the island's [[workbenches]] is one of these.

## tradingPost

A trading post's hall: a counter, shelves of goods, crates and barrels, a hearth. The traders at [[places/westwatch]] and [[places/gullrock]] keep one each.

## dock

A wooden pier on piles over the water. Its deck is ground you walk on, high enough above the water to swim under, and where boats are summoned and moored.

## lighthouse

A tapered round tower, white with red bands, its lamp room glowing over the harbour at night.

## windmill

A stone tower windmill whose sails turn in the wind over the farmland.

## well

The round stone well in Lanternwick's square, with its little roof, crank and bucket: where Reeve Hollis stands.

## stable

The stables: open arches to the yard, lattice walls and hay along the back. [[npcs/sela]] keeps them.

## workshop

The wainwright's workshop, with wide arched doors, a cart on the floor, a workbench, an anvil and a grindstone: where [[npcs/bertram]] builds carts.

## boathouse

The shipwright's shed by the docks, open to the slip, with crates, rope and a workbench: where [[npcs/ilsa]] builds boats.

## registrar

The Land Registry: a stone hall with the clerk's desk facing the arched door. [[npcs/quill]] grants land and takes the tax here.

## launchDeck

The glider launch on top of the [[places/windspire]]: a wooden deck with rails on three sides and open at the front, where you run and jump off.

## noticeBoard

A small roofed board of pinned papers in the square.

## housePlot

A plot of land for claim, marked by corner stakes, string and a sign. Claim it with a house blueprint and build on it.

## scarecrow

The scarecrow that claims a farm plot wherever you set it: a cross pole, a sack head under a straw hat, a patched shirt. Where it stands, the plot beside it is yours.

## farmPlot

A patch of dark tilled soil: the ground of a farm plot, where the villagers' own fields and yours are planted.

## lamp

An iron lamp post with a glass lantern, lit at night.

## signpost

A wooden post with arrow boards pointing the way to the island's places.

## bench

A wooden bench to sit and watch the harbour from.

## barrel

A banded wooden barrel.

## crates

A stack of crates.

## haycart

A small two-wheeled cart piled with hay, standing about the farms.

## fence

A split-rail fence along the fields.
