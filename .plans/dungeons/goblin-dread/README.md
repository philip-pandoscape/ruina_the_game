# Goblin's Dread

The first dungeon: a goblin lair hollowed out inside the Windspire. You get in by gliding off the
summit deck into the waterfall on its face and through the hidden portal behind it. Inside are jump
puzzles in the spirit of Super Mario 64, other puzzles, and goblins to fight. It is instanced the way
World of Warcraft instances its dungeons: your party goes into a copy of its own, kept for 30
minutes after everyone leaves.

## Decided

These are the user's answers to the concept round (2026-11-05) and to the build that followed.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Name | **Goblin's Dread** |
| The outside | A spring pool beside the summit's launch deck spills over the lip and falls the full height of the sheer face into a plunge pool; nothing of the tunnel shows from outside, only a faint glow through the water a third of the way down (`concepts/01-outside-chosen-spring-by-the-deck.jpg`) |
| Every room painted | Kept: the landing grotto, the Warren, the Wheelworks, the Updraft Shaft, the Barrel Run, the Cistern, the Sunbeam Hall, the War Camp, the Chief's Hall, the cutaway |
| Older halls under the lair | Keep both layers: the goblins' shanty and machinery near the top, carved stone halls of an older people deeper down (fitting for a game called Ruina) |
| The story | The goblins stole the great lens from the lighthouse at Lanternwick's harbour. The lighthouse has gone dark; the lens opens the sunbeam door, the chief keeps it, and bringing it home lights the lighthouse again |
| Order of work | The way in first, so the glide in could be tried before the rest was planned |
| Instancing | World of Warcraft's rules: enter with your party through a portal into your own copy; the copy stays alive for 30 minutes after you leave |
| Scope of the first build | The way in plus working parties and instances, the copy holding only the inner grotto |

The goblins themselves are another session's work: the Split-Eye war-band is on beta
(`?yard&goblins=6` shows a band in the training yard). The paintings' goblins were stand-ins.
